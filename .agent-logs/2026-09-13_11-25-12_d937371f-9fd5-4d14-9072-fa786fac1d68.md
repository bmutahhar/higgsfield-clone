## Why blank lines weren't highlighted

`eslint-config-prettier` deliberately turns **off** `no-multiple-empty-lines` (and every other formatting rule), on the theory that Prettier handles it. But Prettier only reformats when something *runs* it — it never reports anything to the editor. So extra blank lines fell into a gap: ESLint was told to ignore them, and Prettier was never invoked. That was my omission in the original setup.

Swapped `eslint-config-prettier/flat` → `eslint-plugin-prettier/recommended`, which runs Prettier *as an ESLint rule*. Blank lines now report as `prettier/prettier — Delete ⏎⏎`, underlined in the editor and auto-fixable.
